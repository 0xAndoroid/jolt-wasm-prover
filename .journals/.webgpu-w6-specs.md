## V1 — coop µop-stream Miller port (the #1 lever)

**Pool:** miller device pool ~6.5s @2^22 (every-run, warm), 20µs/pair × ~345K pairs across 42 commit absorbs (8192p) + 26 st8 multipairs (2048–4096p). Side pool: 68 st8 calls ≤1024p currently sub-gate-declined to dory CPU (~2.1 worker-s). U1 closed with: dispatch-shape knobs exhausted, **the only remaining st0 lever is the June coop kernel** (escalation condition met).

**Donor (read, verbatim facts):** `dory-gpu/src/coop.rs` (~660 lines) + `wgsl/coop_pairing.wgsl` (7.3KB). One **32-lane WG per pair runs the ENTIRE ate loop in one dispatch**; the loop is a CPU-built stream of Fq2 µops (`[dst,a,b,op]` quads, 12 op kinds incl. LINE/LOADQ for prepared/computed paths) over a 56-slot file in `var<workgroup>` (3.5KB packed); groups of ≤32 ops with in-group RAW/WAW asserts in the builder; **one `workgroupBarrier()` per group; ONE `fq2_mul` call site** — deliberately tiny body (the donor comment cites exactly the Apple/naga large-kernel class). Schedule mirrors fq6_mul/fq6_mul_by_01 → Fq12 sqr + mul_by_034 over arkworks' ate schedule — **bit-matched ark by construction**. Two static schedules (prepared + computed). `COOP_MAX_PAIRS=512` with rationale verbatim: below = sequential pipeline dispatch-bound (~450 dispatches dominate); above = per-thread full occupancy + register-resident fq6 chains beat ~1/3 lane utilization.

**Why June's threshold flips at our burst sizes (the scaling answer):** June's coop regime (≤512 pairs = ≤16K threads) was UNDER-saturated — that occupancy penalty, not the µop shape, is what lost above 512 natively. Our absorbs are 8192-pair bursts: coop = 8192 WGs × 32 lanes = **262K threads = 100% of the W1d saturation ladder**; @2^20's 2048-pair absorbs = 65K threads = 97%; st8's 512–1024p = 16–32K = 83–88%. Meanwhile the ">512 winner" (per-thread pipeline) measures 7–8× off roof in browser (per-dispatch f/scratch DRAM round-trips — U1's structural finding). **Multi-WG per pair is NOT needed** — pair-per-WG parallelism saturates at every production call size; 32-lane WG = one simdgroup ⇒ barriers ≈ free. Per-dispatch wall @8192p ≈ 34M muls / (0.865G × util) ≈ 80–250ms — no TDR/segmenting concern; keep ≤8192p per dispatch (hook contract) ⇒ 65535-wg trivially safe. June's own "browser coop-everywhere changed nothing" datum is from their broken-engine era (orchestration was their 27× killer; W1d/W2a exonerated field core + composition) — do NOT port their engine, only kernel + schedule builder.

**Shape compliance:** no EC add bodies at all (≤2-add-class rule vacuously satisfied; interpreter is the slimmest possible body). Barrier-dense interpreter IS a new shape class on this die ⇒ tiny-first probe mandatory. naga fq6 quarantine ⇒ Tint-only serve (wasm32; `miller_serve_native` stays false), coop KATs join the naga quarantine list, Tint vectors = truth.
